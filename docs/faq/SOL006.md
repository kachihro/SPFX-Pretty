# SOL006: Long description missing, a placeholder, or the same as the short one

**Area:** App Catalog · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

Shown on the app details page. Admins read it to decide whether to deploy the app.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.metadata.longDescription.default`

## How to fix

1. Click "Write the long description…".
2. Cover: what it does, who it is for, any setup (lists, permissions) and who to contact.
