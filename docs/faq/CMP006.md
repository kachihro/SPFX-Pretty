# CMP006: Web part is in the wrong toolbox group

**Area:** Toolbox · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

The modern toolbox only has 7 sections. Any other groupId (e.g. a company "Personal" group) falls back to "Advanced", where authors look last.

## Where

`<WebPart>.manifest.json → preconfiguredEntries[].groupId`

## How to fix

1. Click "Choose toolbox group…" and pick the closest section (Discovery, Communication and collaboration, …).
2. A company group name only shows on classic pages - use a title prefix ("Kachihro …") to keep your web parts together.
