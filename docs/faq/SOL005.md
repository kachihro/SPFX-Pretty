# SOL005: Short description missing or a placeholder

**Area:** App Catalog · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

Shown under the app title in the App Catalog and "Add an app". The generator writes "my-app description".

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.metadata.shortDescription.default`

## How to fix

1. Click "Write the short description…" and type one friendly sentence.
2. Say what it does for the user, not how it is built.

### Before

```json
"default": "project description"
```

### After

```json
"default": "Quick links to the tools your team uses every day."
```
