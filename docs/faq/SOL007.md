# SOL007: No categories

**Area:** App Catalog · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

Categories help people filter apps in the App Catalog.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.metadata.categories`

## How to fix

1. Click "Add categories…" and enter a comma-separated list, e.g. "Productivity, Content management".
