# SOL015: App icon is the generated placeholder

**Area:** Icons · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

The brand-colour icon SPFX Pretty generates is a stop-gap. Your company icon is far more recognisable.

## How to fix

1. Click "Upload company icon (96x96)…" and choose your logo PNG.
