# DEF002: Property pane text is the generator default

**Area:** Defaults · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

Authors editing the web part see "Description", "Group Name" and "Description Field" - they say nothing about your settings.

## Where

`src/webparts/<name>/loc/en-us.js (and every other locale file)`

## How to fix

1. Rename the header, group and field labels to describe your settings, e.g. "Choose which links to show".
2. Update every loc/*.js file, not just en-us.
