# CMP004: Both officeFabricIconFontName and iconImageUrl are set

**Area:** Toolbox · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

SharePoint uses the Fluent icon when both are set, so your image is silently ignored.

## How to fix

1. Click the fix to remove officeFabricIconFontName and keep your image.
