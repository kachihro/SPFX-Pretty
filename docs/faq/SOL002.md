# SOL002: solution.title is not a nice title

**Area:** App Catalog · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

The optional title should read like a product name, the same as solution.name.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.title`

## How to fix

1. Apply the suggested title, or remove "title" and rely on "name".
