# SOL014: App name does not start with your prefix

**Area:** App Catalog · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

A common prefix (e.g. "Kachihro - ") keeps all your organisation's apps together in the App Catalog and Site Contents.

## How to fix

1. Apply the fix, or change the prefix in Settings → SPFX Pretty → App Name Prefix.
