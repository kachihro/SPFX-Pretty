# SOL008: No screenshots on the app details page

**Area:** App Catalog · **Needs you**

## Why it matters

A screenshot shows admins and site owners what they are adding before they deploy it.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.metadata.screenshotPaths (files in ./sharepoint)`

## How to fix

1. Take 1-5 screenshots of the web part on a real page (PNG/JPG, around 1366x768).
2. Save them in sharepoint/images/, e.g. sharepoint/images/screenshot1.png.
3. List them in package-solution.json (paths relative to ./sharepoint).
4. Rebuild and upload the .sppkg.
5. Not needed? Right-click → Ignore This Rule.

### Example

```json
"screenshotPaths": [
  "images/screenshot1.png",
  "images/screenshot2.png"
]
```
