# BRD004: Hard-coded font-family

**Area:** Branding · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

Brand Center fonts chosen in Change the look won't apply.

## How to fix

1. Apply the fix to use a Brand Center font slot.

### After

```
font-family: var(--fontFamilyCustomFont800, var(--fontFamilyBase));
```
