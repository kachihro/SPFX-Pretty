# TMS001: Teams icons missing

**Area:** Teams · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

Web parts exposed as Teams tabs or personal apps need teams/{componentId}_color.png (192x192) and _outline.png (32x32), or Teams shows a generic icon.

## How to fix

1. Click the fix (or Upload Company Logo) to create both files from your logo.
