# ABT001: No About section in the property pane

**Area:** About · **Guided fix (asks you)**

## Why it matters

Authors and support staff can't see the version, developer or loaded packages when something goes wrong.

## How to fix

1. Apply Fix adds src/common/about/ (logo, title, version, developer, website, runtime packages) and wires aboutGroup(this.context.manifest) into the property pane.
