# SOL001: App name is not a nice title

**Area:** App Catalog · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

solution.name is the title people read in the App Catalog, "Add an app" and Site Contents. A package-style name like "project-client-side-solution" looks unfinished.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.name`

## How to fix

1. Click the fix to rename it to the suggested title, or type your own.
2. Rebuild (heft package-solution --production) and upload the new .sppkg over the old one.
3. It is safe: SharePoint tracks the app by solution.id, not the name.

### Before

```json
"name": "project-client-side-solution"
```

### After

```json
"name": "Kachihro Project"
```
