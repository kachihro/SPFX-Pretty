# SOL012: App icon is the wrong format or size

**Area:** Icons · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

The App Catalog expects a square PNG; 96x96 looks crisp.

## How to fix

1. Upload a square PNG of at least 96x96 - SPFX Pretty resizes it.
