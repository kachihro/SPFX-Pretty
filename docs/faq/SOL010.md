# SOL010: No app icon

**Area:** Icons · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

Without solution.iconPath the App Catalog, "Add an app" and Site Contents show a generic placeholder, so your app looks like everyone else's.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.iconPath (relative to ./sharepoint)`

## How to fix

1. Click "Upload company icon (96x96)…" and pick a square PNG - it is resized and wired up for you.
2. Or run "SPFX Pretty: Upload Company Logo" once and it is remembered for every project.
3. Rebuild and upload the .sppkg.

### After

```
"iconPath": "images/AppIcon.png"   →  file: sharepoint/images/AppIcon.png (96x96 PNG)
```
