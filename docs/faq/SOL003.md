# SOL003: Feature title is the generator default

**Area:** App Catalog · **Auto-fix available**

## Why it matters

Site owners see feature titles in Site settings → Manage site features. "my-app Feature" looks unfinished.

## Where

`config/package-solution.json → solution.features[].title`

## How to fix

1. Apply the suggested "<App name> Feature", or write your own.
